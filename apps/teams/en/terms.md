## 1. What this tool does

dira hands an agent CLI (`claude` and the like) the job of reading and changing your files.
You pick the directory that becomes a queue root, you judge what gets committed inside it,
and you decide whether to accept what comes out. The Company never reaches into your files.
The engine and the workers run on your Mac, with your permissions.

This clause states a fact before it disclaims anything. In a directory you register as a
queue root, an agent that holds write permission there can go as far as making commits. Pick
the queue root knowing that.
